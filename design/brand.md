# Brand: logo mark, app icon, store and web

Owner: `designer`. Files in `design/brand/`, `design/store/`, `docs/legal/`.
Colours come from `tokens.json` (3.0.0); there are no brand-only colours.
Generated by `design/tools/brand_assets.py` (edit the geometry there, never the
SVGs by hand); review PNGs by `design/tools/brand_previews.py` into
`design/brand/previews/`.

## Questions and decisions

- **OQ-D37 — decided: look approved by Sabit 2026-11-19.**
  The brand moves from the v2 violet/pink palette to v3 "dəftər": ink mark on
  paper, one nar accent, Fraunces wordmark. Covers the mark, the adaptive icon
  (all three layers), the splash icon, the notification icon and accent, the Play
  feature graphic, the screenshot caption frames and the web legal pages. Until
  Sabit approves, android-dev may build it behind nothing (it is only resources)
  but should not upload a release with it. Review: `design/brand/previews/*.png`.
- **OQ-D38 — decided by Sabit 2026-11-19: paper launcher background.** Proposal: paper
  (reasons under "App icon"). Alternative: ink background with a paper bubble and
  the nar bubble (`#EC7B6F`), the dark-theme mark on a dark tile. Decides: Sabit.
- **OQ-D39 — decided by Sabit 2026-11-19: local/system fonts only, no web font download.** Proposal: keep "no
  external requests" (system serif and sans, Fraunces / Plus Jakarta Sans only if
  installed). Google Fonts is rejected: it would send every visitor's IP address
  to Google, which the privacy policy says does not happen ("no other third
  parties"). Alternative: self-host subset WOFF files of both fonts in
  `docs/legal/fonts/` (same origin, about 60 KB in total, OFL text alongside), for
  the exact brand type. Decides: Sabit.
- OQ-D15 (approved 2026-10-16) still holds: the mark is two speech bubbles, a
  question between two players. v3 keeps the concept and redraws it.
- Decided: no wordmark file. "Duello Quiz" is set in **Fraunces SemiBold**
  (`type.headlineMedium` and larger) next to or under the mark; text is never
  baked into the app icon. Only the feature graphic carries the name as outlines.

## v3 — the idea

The ink bubble asks the question; the nar bubble behind it is the other player
answering back, tails pointing away from each other like two people across a
table. It is drawn like a printed page, not a game button: flat ink, a serif
question mark from Fraunces, brush-curved tails instead of triangles, and the
accent used once. The nar shape is the only colour, so the icon stays calm on a
busy home screen and still says "duel" by its two voices.

## Geometry (108-unit design space)

- Front bubble: rounded rectangle x 26–66, y 28–64, radius 12; its lower-left
  corner becomes a brush-curved tail ending at (25, 76).
- Back bubble: rounded rectangle x 50–88, y 38–74, radius 11; its lower-right
  corner becomes the mirrored tail ending at (89, 86). Only its visible part is
  drawn: the bubble minus the front bubble grown by a **3-unit gap** (so no
  masks, no background-coloured "gap ring"; it works on any surface).
- `?`: the Fraunces SemiBold glyph (opsz 72, SOFT 50, as bundled) as outlines,
  21 units tall, centred on (46, 46.5).
- Launcher layers: the mark is scaled 0.781 around the centre of its smallest
  enclosing circle (59.25, 58.75) and moved to (54, 54). Farthest point 31.5
  units from the centre: inside the 33-unit (66dp) safe zone with 1.5 units to
  spare, so circle, squircle, rounded-square and teardrop masks never cut it.
- In-app mark files: square viewBox `24 24 66 66` around the shapes.

## Clear space and minimum sizes

- Clear space around the mark: **1/4 of the mark's width** on every side; the
  wordmark counts as a separate element (gap between mark and wordmark = 1/4 of
  the mark width; wordmark font size ≈ 0.4 × mark height, e.g. 100px next to the
  238px mark on the feature graphic).
- Minimum size of the colour mark: **20dp** (the `?` stays readable). Below
  that (favicon 16px) use `app-icon-full.svg`, never the bare mark.
- Notification small icon: 24dp only (the system size).
- The app icon is checked at 48dp on light and dark launchers
  (`previews/launcher-light-dark.png`) and in all masks plus themed icons
  (`previews/adaptive-masks-themed.png`).

## Do / don't

Do:
- Put the mark on `scheme.surface` or any surface container of the same theme;
  use the light file in light theme and the dark file in dark theme.
- Keep the ink bubble in front and the nar bubble behind, as drawn.
- Set "Duello Quiz" in Fraunces SemiBold, sentence case, ink.

Don't:
- No gradients, shadows, outlines around the mark, 3D, glow or textures.
- Don't recolour the bubbles (no category colours, no saffron, no plum), don't
  swap them, don't rotate or tilt the mark (the rotated sticker is a UI element,
  not a brand one).
- Don't put the light mark on ink or on a photo; use the dark file or the
  full icon.
- Don't add hand-drawn touches to the mark itself. The squiggle may appear under
  **one word** of a tagline (feature graphic), never under the name.
- Never coins, chips, dice, cards, crowns, trophies, slot shapes, "VS" lightning,
  sparkles, confetti.

## App icon

- **Background: paper `#F5EFE3` in both system themes.** Reasons: (1) the ink
  bubble is the figure; on paper it has 15:1 contrast and reads at 48dp, while on
  an ink tile the front bubble would have to turn paper and the mark would read
  as a white blob among white icons; (2) paper is the app's first frame (splash
  and welcome, light theme), so the icon and the app match; (3) a warm cream tile
  is distinct both on dark wallpapers and next to the pure-white tiles most apps
  use. On a pale wallpaper the tile edge is soft, but the ink and nar shapes carry
  the icon (see the preview). Alternative in OQ-D38.
- Foreground colours are fixed (an adaptive icon is not themed except through
  the monochrome layer).
- Themed icons (Android 13+): the system draws `ic-launcher-monochrome.svg` in
  its own two tones; both bubbles become one colour separated by the gap, the
  `?` is a hole.

## Notification icon and accent

- **Small icon changes**: `ic_stat_duello` is redrawn from
  `brand/ic-stat-duello.svg` (white v3 silhouette, `?` as a hole, gap between
  the bubbles). Android uses only alpha.
- **Accent** (`com.google.firebase.messaging.default_notification_color`):
  `@color/notification_accent` = `#A82D25` (nar, light) in `values/`, `#EC7B6F`
  (nar, dark) in `values-night/`. Contrast against the shade: 5.56 on a light
  shade (`#E7E7EC`), 5.07 on a dark shade (`#2B2C30`); the light nar alone on a
  dark shade would be 2.04, hence the night value. Android 12+ may still adjust
  the tint for contrast; that is fine.
- No large icon, no image (unchanged, `screens/notifications.md`).

## Splash (Android 12+)

- Light theme: background `scheme.surface` light, icon = adaptive foreground.
- Dark theme: background `scheme.surface` dark; the light foreground (ink
  bubble) would vanish there, so `values-night` points the splash icon to
  `splash-icon-dark.svg` (same geometry, dark colours). No icon background
  colour.
- Which variant shows follows the night mode the splash theme resolves with
  (`screens/splash-welcome.md`, SET-01 rule); both variants are complete, so
  either is correct on its own background.
